# Your Invoices

After you purchase a license for Super Forms, you will receive your invoice per E-mail. It will be attached as a PDF file.

All invoices can be found in the dashboard under **Invoices** (Quick Actions) at [dashboard.super-forms.com](https://dashboard.super-forms.com). The page title is **Invoices**; your list is under **Your Invoices**.

{% hint style="info" %}
From WordPress, **Super Forms > Licenses** opens a shortcut to the same dashboard. **Invoices** is its own Quick Action — it is not under **Manage Billing**.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="warning" %}
In order to view your Invoices you will have to login with your Super Forms account first!
{% endhint %}
