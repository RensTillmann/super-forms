# Billing details

### Updating your billing details

To change your billing details, open [dashboard.super-forms.com](https://dashboard.super-forms.com) and sign in with your [Super Forms account](../../quick-start/registration.md#registering-a-new-account). Click **Billing**. Here you can update your existing billing details.

### Updating your card details

To update an existing card or add a new one, open [dashboard.super-forms.com](https://dashboard.super-forms.com), sign in with your [Super Forms account](../../quick-start/registration.md#registering-a-new-account), and open **Billing**. Under "Payment methods" click the blue button "Add or edit payment method".
