# Billing details

### Updating your billing details

To change your billing details, open [dashboard.super-forms.com](https://dashboard.super-forms.com) and sign in with your [Super Forms account](../../quick-start/registration.md#registering-a-new-account). Open **Manage Billing** (Quick Actions). This action shows after your first purchase. The page title is **Billing Details** — update your details there.

### Changing the card you pay with

The dashboard lets you add and remove cards. To switch to a different card:

1. Open [dashboard.super-forms.com](https://dashboard.super-forms.com) and sign in with your [Super Forms account](../../quick-start/registration.md#registering-a-new-account).
2. Open **Payment Methods** (Quick Actions).
3. Click **Add Card** (or **Add Your First Card** if you have none yet). Enter the details under **Card Details** and click **Add Card**.
4. On the new card, click **Set as Default**. This makes it the card for your account and for every active subscription.
5. Optional: to remove the old card, click **Remove** on it, then **Yes, Remove**.

Your first card becomes the default automatically. Any card you add later is only saved until you click **Set as Default**, so the old card keeps being charged until you do. If you remove the default card, another saved card is made the default for you.
