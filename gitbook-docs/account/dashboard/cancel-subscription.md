# Cancel subscription

Open [dashboard.super-forms.com](https://dashboard.super-forms.com) and sign in with your [Super Forms account](../../quick-start/registration.md#registering-a-new-account) (WordPress **Super Forms > Licenses** opens the same place). Click **Cancel Subscription**.

In case you no longer have access to your WordPress site you can contact us directly by submitting a [support ticket](https://f4d.nl/super-forms/support/) and providing the Super Forms account (email address) and which site(s) you wish to unsubscribe to.
