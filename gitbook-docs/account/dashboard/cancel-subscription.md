# Cancel subscription

Open [dashboard.super-forms.com](https://dashboard.super-forms.com) and sign in with your [Super Forms account](../../quick-start/registration.md#registering-a-new-account).

1. On the home page, find the site under **Your Websites** and expand its card.
2. On the subscription licence row, click **Cancel Subscription**.
3. Confirm with **Yes, Cancel Subscription**.

{% hint style="info" %}
From WordPress, **Super Forms > Licenses** opens a shortcut to the same dashboard. Unused subscription codes (not yet tied to a site) use **Cancel License** on the unused-code card instead.
{% endhint %}

In case you no longer have access to your WordPress site you can contact us directly by submitting a [support ticket](https://f4d.nl/super-forms/support/) and providing the Super Forms account (email address) and which site(s) you wish to unsubscribe to.
